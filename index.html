About

I'm Babar, an aspiring product designer based in London, UK. My interest in design started years ago when I worked at ProgressTeaching. I got a lot of joy from speaking to customers, deeply understanding their problems, and designing solutions to fix them.

Making a material impact on a person's experience is what drives me, and with over 8 years of speaking with users, I've developed a lot of empathy for the friction they face.

Being a massive nerd also helps... I LOVE getting into the nitty-gritty of technical problems which means I don't shy away from responsibility and owning things end-to-end.

In my free time, I play piano, climb rocks, and design games.

Feel free to reach out to me to chat about anything design or if you are interested in collaborating on projects at b.sattar@gmail.com
