Five Platform Papercuts, Shipped from my CSM Role

Small UX fixes I identified, prototyped and shipped to production using Claude Code, all from my role as a Customer Success Manager.

Design Judgement Claude Code Shipped to Production

Timeline

2026

Role

CSM (self-initiated)

Outcome

5 fixes in production

The pattern

I'm in the product every day. That's how I found these.

I'm in Gearset every day, customer calls, live troubleshooting, and walkthroughs of different product areas. That means I hit the same small frictions our users hit.

To address these issues, I went and got access to Claude Code and Gearset's codebase (which took approval and persistence).

I learned the workflows used by the UX team, then built and shipped five fixes to production.

Deep dive

Delegate org access: a table that wouldn't scale.

Before Save button hidden at the bottom of the table

31 users listed in full. The Save button sits below every row.

After Save button always visible

126 users in a scrollable table. Save is always visible.

Fine at ten users. Not fine at a hundred and twenty six.

In our product Slack channels I saw that Gearset's largest customer was hitting problems the platform hadn't been designed for (such as assigning licences for a team over 100 people).

Later, I independently noticed the delegate org access page listed every user on the team in one long list. To save, you scrolled past all of them.

I found an existing table component in the platform and reused it here. I raised the PR, worked with Engineering on some small UI tweaks, and it's in production now.

The other fixes

Three more, caught from daily use.

Search term highlighting, What's New

I searched for a release note and then had to scan the results to find my own search term. Now matches are highlighted.

Before Search results weren't highlighted and hard to find

After Search results highlighted and immediately identifiable

Clickable card area, Observability

The card looked clickable but only part of it was, so clicks landed on dead space. Now the whole card is a target.

Before Unclickable dead space on the card

After Full card is clickable

Agent resizing handle

The agent window was resizable but the interaction was hidden. Now it's discoverable.

Before Resizing is completely hidden

After Resizing handle is discoverable and has a hover state

The process

How a CSM ships code at Gearset.

Spot it.

I identify friction from daily product use, log a ticket in Shortcut, and self-assign it.

1

Build it.

I create a feature branch, prototype different approaches with Claude Code, and raise a PR.

2

Ship it.

I work through Engineering's code review, test in Staging, iterate, and ship to production.

3

The ceiling

I've found the limit of what my CSM role allows.

From my CSM role I can spot friction and ship a fix, but I can't take on more meaningful and higher-impact problems.

I've found the limit of what I can do as a CSM, the work I want to do is that of a dedicated Product Designer.
